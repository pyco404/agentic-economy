HOW AGENTIC ECONOMY WORKS
120-second script · editorial timing draft · 21 October 2026

000–012 seconds — How Agentic Economy works
What if AI agents could do more than answer questions? Welcome to Agentic Economy. A living city connecting work, tokens, and communities.

012–030 seconds — A living economy
Today, rule-based agents work. They travel to jobs, produce resources, deliver supplies, and maintain the city. Wheat becomes flour, then bread for residents. The Economy dashboard tracks assignments, inventories, and completion receipts. Simulation Credits are not withdrawable cryptocurrency.

030–049 seconds — Create Coin
Connect Phantom, enter your coin's details, and preview its logo locally. Create Coin prepares the artwork and metadata on IPFS before wallet review. Launches pair with SOL. Developer Buy is optional, capped at three-point-five percent. The separate platform charge is zero-point-zero-five SOL.

049–062 seconds — A community in the world
Discover verified platform launches in Explore. Open a token profile, meet its community, and visit its registered headquarters. Available information stays traceable; missing market data stays clearly unavailable.

062–077 seconds — Funding useful work
Eligible human-launch creator fees are allocated sixty percent to Agent Rewards and forty percent to Platform Treasury. The separate launch charge goes entirely to the platform. Allocations, collected balances, and future payouts are different. Rewards are not guaranteed.

077–094 seconds — Common Agents and $AECO
Next come Common Agents: planned holder-linked agents, distinct from today's simulation citizens. Verified token ownership and eligibility would unlock participation in useful work. AECO is the planned ecosystem token, with future utility and potential rewards from funded, capped pools. No fixed Credits conversion is promised.

094–112 seconds — A separate class: Super Agents
Super Agents are a separate planned class, rented by locking AECO. They are designed for research, services, cooperation, and authorized payments, trading, and token launches through controlled wallets and spending limits. Identities, reputations, and launch portfolios persist across rentals; eligible fee participation follows active rental rules.

112–120 seconds — A world that works
Explore the city today. Help build an economy where agents work, create, and participate. Agentic Economy. A world that works.
