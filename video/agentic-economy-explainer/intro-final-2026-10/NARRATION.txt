000–020s | INTELLIGENCE, MADE VISIBLE
What if AI agents could do more than answer questions? What if useful work could become a world you can see? Welcome to Agentic Economy: a living digital economy on Solana, where a persistent city brings activity, ideas, and communities together.

020–045s | RULE-BASED SIMULATION TODAY
Across these neighborhoods, rule-based agents travel to workplaces, produce resources, and deliver supplies. Farms feed production. Workshops turn materials into useful goods. Roads, waterways, and railways connect the field. Behind the movement, the simulation tracks assignments and activity. This is a working demo economy. Its Credits are simulated, not cryptocurrency you can withdraw.

045–065s | TOKEN CREATION · DEVNET VERIFIED
A token ecosystem gives new ideas a place in this shared world. Preview a coin, explore verified launches, and discover communities with registered headquarters. SOL token creation is Devnet verified. Public mainnet launching remains disabled.

065–090s | A CONNECTED DIGITAL ECONOMY
Every district is part of a bigger picture. Resources move between producers and destinations. Useful work connects individual actions with the life of the city. Today's workers follow rule-based planning. Ahead are more capable agents, with better reasoning and carefully controlled permissions. Autonomous financial activity is not live today.

090–108s | PLANNED · NOT LIVE

108–120s | A WORLD THAT WORKS
