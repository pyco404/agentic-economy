AGENTIC ECONOMY — UPDATED INTRODUCTION
Six-scene 120-second narration draft; timing to be measured after sample approval.

0–20 seconds: A world that works.
What if AI agents could do more than answer questions? A living digital world, built around useful work. Welcome to Agentic Economy. A persistent city where neighborhoods, communities, and economic activity become part of one shared world.

20–45 seconds: A city that works.
Across its streets, rule-based simulation agents travel to workplaces, produce resources, and move supplies. Farms connect with productive facilities. Roads, rivers, and railways link the neighborhoods. Agentic Economy is building a digital world where contributions matter, and where useful work gives the city its rhythm. Today's Credits are simulated, not withdrawable cryptocurrency.

45–65 seconds: Ideas find a home.
Built on Solana, the token ecosystem connects new ideas with a place in the city. Preview a coin, explore verified launches, and discover the communities behind them. Token creation has been tested on Devnet. Public mainnet launching remains disabled while the platform develops.

65–90 seconds: Participation, made visible.
The agents you see today belong to the simulation. Ahead are Common Agents: planned holder-linked participants in useful economic work. Super Agents are a separate future class, designed for advanced reasoning, services, and cooperation. Their financial abilities are planned, with controlled wallets and permissions. They are not operating autonomously with real funds today.

90–108 seconds: An economy that can grow.
The longer-term vision connects intelligent services, coordinated agents, and future AECO utility. Super Agents would keep persistent identities and reputations across rentals, accessed by locking AECO. Participation and potential rewards would depend on eligibility, useful contributions, and funded limits. Nothing promises profits or guaranteed payouts.

108–120 seconds: Agentic Economy.
This future starts with a world you can explore today. A place for agents to work, communities to grow, and ideas to take shape. Agentic Economy. A world that works.
